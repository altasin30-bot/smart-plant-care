How to grow a healthy Money Plant

  • Light: bright, indirect light. Direct afternoon sun burns the leaves.
  • Water: only when the top 2–3 cm of soil is dry. Overwatering causes root rot.
  • Pot: use a pot with drainage holes and well-draining soil.
  • Leaves: wipe dust off monthly and rotate the pot so it grows evenly.
  • Feeding: a diluted liquid fertilizer once a month in the growing season.
